Weekly Highlights
Performance Marketing
  • Meta spend roughly tripled to €2,888.90 (W42: €968.34) for 2.09M impressions and 28,247 clicks. CRSL_1_BeautyofJoseon alone took €979.94, a third of the week’s spend.
  • Catalog ads carried conversions: Maison_segmented_catalog drove 18 website purchases at €22.54 each with a 6.19% CTR, and the Fashion collection returned a 5.4x purchase ROAS.
  • CRSL_1_BeautyofJoseon is the least efficient of the top 5, 10 purchases at €97.99 each and a 0.26 purchase ROAS, worth a review before budgets scale for the first Flash Sale weekend (6–8 Nov).
  • Installs held at 1,877 (+3%) with paid flat at 1,553. Organic grew 21% to 324, driven by iOS (207, up from 140).
  • Traffic rose on both channels (web sessions +28%, app +10%) and add-to-cart sessions jumped 48% to 1,202, but only 5.5% of sessions with a product view add to cart, the funnel’s weakest step.
Page 03 Overview / Ops

Overview & Ops

Week ending 27 October 2026, excluding routine digital-performance reporting: weekly highlights, partnerships, the plan of action, and current blocking points.

Weekly Highlights
  • Black Friday / Wasal Friday: Moved into active launch mode. Teasers are live and the November execution architecture is now structured around four Flash Sale weekends: 6–8, 13–15, 20–22 and WOW Friday 27–29 November. The required month-long KV, countdown stories, product carousels and Sales product-input structure have been created.
  • Campaign readiness: Not yet green. Week 1 and Week 2 deliverables are being verified.
  • Offline activation: Blocked. Mall screens and potential mall ad spaces are under question.
  • Production governance: Continues to improve. The production audit and tracking system remain active, with briefing quality established as the first WoW (week-over-week) improvement priority against the 30% bottleneck baseline. No new comparative percentage has yet been validated this week.
  • Operating model: Being standardised. The Activation Playbook is developed, the teams are finalising the inputs, and all activations must be emailed team-wide, including to the CRC team, by the end of the week.
  • Team operating system: Becoming more disciplined. The four November Flash Sale tasks, the product-input process and the cross-board Sales × W-Studio architecture are now structured. The remaining issue is converting the structure into consistently completed and visible work.
