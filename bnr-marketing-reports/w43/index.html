Weekly Highlights
Performance Marketing
  • Installs held steady at 1,877 (+3%). Paid installs were flat at 1,553, so the gain came from organic: 324 (+21%), driven by iOS (207, up from 140). Organic share rose to 17.3% of installs, from 14.7%.
  • App engagement edged up: 4,415 active users (+7%) and 6,573 app sessions (+10%), with 59% of sessions coming from returning visitors (58% in W42).
  • 349 app sessions reached a successful add to cart (+20% from 290), 5.3% of app sessions against 4.9% in W42, and 44 ended in a purchase (+38% from 32).
  • Meta spend roughly tripled to €2,888.90 (W42: €968.34) for 2.09M impressions and 28,247 clicks. CRSL_1_BeautyofJoseon alone took €979.94, a third of it, for 10 purchases at €97.99 each (purchase ROAS 0.26), the least efficient ad in the top 5.
  • Catalog ads were the most efficient: Maison_segmented_catalog drove 18 website purchases at €22.54 each with a 6.19% CTR, and the Fashion collection returned a 5.4x purchase ROAS. The Beauty of Joseon carousel’s share of budget is worth a review before the first Flash Sale weekend (6–8 Nov).
Page 03 Overview / Ops

Overview & Ops

Week ending 27 October 2026, excluding routine digital-performance reporting: Wasal Friday launch readiness, offline activation, production governance, the operating model and partnerships, the plan of action, and current blocking points.

Weekly Highlights
General overview
  • Black Friday / Wasal Friday moved into active launch mode. Teasers are live and the November execution architecture is now structured around four Flash Sale weekends: 6–8, 13–15, 20–22 and WOW Friday 27–29 November. The required month-long KV, countdown stories, product carousels and Sales product-input structure have been created.
  • Campaign readiness is not yet green. Week 1 and Week 2 deliverables are being verified.
  • Offline activation is blocked. Mall screens and potential mall ad spaces are under question.
  • Production governance continues to improve. The production audit and tracking system remain active, with briefing quality established as the first WoW (week-over-week) improvement priority against the 30% bottleneck baseline. No new comparative percentage has yet been validated this week.
  • The operating model is being standardised: the Activation Playbook is developed, the teams are finalising the inputs, and all activations must be emailed team-wide, including to the CRC team, by the end of the week.
  • Partnership work progressed:
    • Orange: activation is progressing through a landing page with phone validation and up to 1,000 unique vouchers. Fraud controls and non-cumulative voucher logic still need to be locked.
    • AWB: quick wins agreed; AWB’s timeline is a minimum of 2 weeks. We send our Brand Guidelines and assets, AWB sends us the activation timeline.
    • Yallad: framework in negotiation. Email sent, awaiting response.
    • CDM: initial outreach is done, follow-up by the end of the week.
  • The team operating system is becoming more disciplined: the four November Flash Sale tasks, the product-input process and the cross-board Sales × W-Studio architecture are now structured. The remaining issue is converting the structure into consistently completed and visible work.

Plan of Action

Wasal Friday GO Decision
  • Hold the final readiness review and make a documented GO decision based on: commercial offer and stock confirmation; hero-product and replacement logic; approved copy and claims; App/Web landing pages; CRM, Social, Paid Media, Offline and Partner readiness; operational fulfilment and Customer Care readiness.
Immediate Team Gates
  • Confirm teasing channels live.
  • Close Beauty Station GO/NO-GO by 30 October.
  • Confirm Launch Day channels by 3 November.
  • Resolve City Fashion rework and obtain its publication decision.

  • Badr will present the Paid Media investment per audience segment for BF, to explain the budget allocation and give COMEX better visibility and understanding.
Universe Landing Pages
  • Landing pages per universe are being finalised, awaiting complete wireframes with creatives.
Activation Playbook
  • Finalise the Activation Playbook and launch the first cross-functional pilot.
Sales on Monday
  • Complete Sales onboarding on Monday.com, with Sales accountable for updating product, stock, offer, price and validity inputs directly.
Production Audit
  • Run the next production audit and compare briefing-related delays against the 30% baseline, then report the first validated WoW improvement.
Partnerships
  • Confirm Yallad implementation next steps.
  • Continue Orange contract and fraud-control validation.
  • Restart the CDM partnership discussion.
  • Launch the AWB partnership timeline.
