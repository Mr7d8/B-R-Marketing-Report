Weekly Highlights
Performance Marketing
  • Installs rose 14% to 2,141. Paid (1,773) and organic (368) grew at the same pace, so the organic share held at 17.2% (17.3% in W43). Android drove the organic gain, 155 installs against 117, while iOS organic was steady at 213.
  • App engagement was flat: 4,558 active users (+1%) and 6,590 app sessions (−2%). Returning visitors made 56% of app sessions, down from 60% in W43.
  • Intent improved deeper in the app: 376 sessions reached a successful add to cart (+8%), 5.7% of app sessions against 5.2%, and 191 started a checkout (+14% from 167). Purchase sessions slipped to 42 (W43: 44).
  • Meta spend rose 9% to €3,143.07 for 2.87M impressions and 47,874 clicks (1.67% CTR). Catalog ads did most of the work: the DPA drove 41 website purchases at €19.28 each (purchase ROAS 1.58), and the five catalog and collection ads together took 37% of spend for 64 of the 92 website purchases from ads that ran this week.
  • Several carousels converted at a much higher cost: in the Black Friday promo campaign, FASHION-H_CARR01 spent €210.38 without a purchase and KIDS_CARR_01 paid €46.97 per purchase, while FEMME_CRSL_01 paid €94.29. Another €527.25 went to Wasal Friday and Flash Sale teasers on engagement objectives (9,645 post interactions, 249,438 ThruPlays) ahead of the first Flash Sale weekend (6–8 Nov).
Page 03 Overview / Ops

Overview & Ops

Wasal Friday recap of 3 November 2026, excluding routine digital-performance reporting: launch readiness for the first Flash Sale (6–8 November), paid media and creative, influencers and partnerships, production and team capacity, the plan of action, and current blocking points.

Weekly Highlights
General overview
  • Black Friday execution is moving into the launch phase, with the first Flash Sale scheduled for 6–8 November. The campaign governance and control structure is active in Monday, but execution remains highly dependent on accurate commercial inputs, final creative handoff and rapid cross-functional validation.
  • Paid media assets include DPAs and product carousels with specific offers. The hero architecture keeps changing, as products are removed or go out of stock during campaign prep.
  • Landing-page issues have been identified and are being dealt with rapidly: 90% of the problems identified are already resolved. Wiame (Marketing) monitors the app and all of its functionalities daily.
  • The Wasal Friday creative system with the new Brand Identity is being extended across the main key visual, homepage, landing pages, push notifications, pop-ups and Festival des Marques, with much more coherent results.
  • Influencer activation is progressing: 9 of 10 creators have been negotiated and activated, and the codes and creator list are to be sent to Mehdi in the Customer Relationship Centre. 24K of the 150K budget originally dedicated remains.
  • AWB partnership materials are being finalised and will be sent by email by end of day, including the content plan, KV formats and deeplinks.
  • W Moments, social challenges, UGC and W Partners content are being integrated into the Black Friday ecosystem, especially on the Home Page.
  • Hero-product Shoot 2 must be organised urgently for Weeks 2 and 3; products remain a blocking issue for it.
  • The Thursday Inditex shooting test is planned with Ossama, Chakib and Chakib’s team. Its scope must be agreed after testing so the marketing plan can be prepared.
  • Hiring: the team is assessing urgent hiring needs following Imad’s departure and the need to reinforce W-Studio capacity.
