Weekly Highlights
Performance Marketing
  • Growth continued for a second straight week: Active Users 7,196 (+22%), Installs 4,615 (+41%) and Sessions 17.97K (+29%).
  • Installs are still paid-driven. Organic Installs held flat at 364 (+1%), so organic fell to 7.9% of all installs, from about 11% the week before.
  • This time conversion kept pace with traffic: Add To Cart rose 60% to 217, faster than sessions, so 1.2% of sessions reached a cart, against about 1.0% the previous week.
  • Engagement per user edged up to 2.5 sessions per active user, from about 2.4.
  • Daily active users held between 1K and 1.3K, peaking at 1.3K on Sep 27 and Sep 28 before easing back to 1K on Sep 29.
  • Day-zero retention improved to 95.51% (+2%, from 93.20%): Android 95.29% (+3%), iOS 99.61% (−6%, from 105.64%).
  • W1 retention for the Sep 14–20 install cohort came in at 7.09%, with iOS far ahead of Android (16.92% against 6.25%). W1 for this week’s larger cohort is not available yet and is the number to watch next week.
Page 03 Overview / Ops

Overview & Ops

Outside digital performance: production workflow, activation, creators and UGC, and partnerships, the plan of action for the weeks ahead, and current blocking points.

Weekly Highlights
General overview
  • We identified the main production bottleneck within Marketing and launched a system to audit and track it across the workflow. Initial analysis shows that 30% of the bottleneck is linked to briefing issues, making briefing quality the first priority for the WoW improvement review.
  • Marketing productivity continues to improve through clearer ownership, workflow visibility and structured follow-up.
  • An internal Activation Playbook has been developed to standardise activation planning and guide all teams through the required process.
  • The Sales team will be onboarded onto Monday to improve cross-functional project visibility and enable them to manage their own projects within the same operating framework.
  • 40K in cash has been unblocked for casting.
  • 5 creators have been contacted to be product seeded with Seller products, to provide UGCs for Paid Ads and animate our Socials. They will create a report on their experience.
  • Content.ma, a UGC marketplace, is being tested from the creator side with Nouhaila, Azza and Mia.
  • Social content is being analysed for click-through rate, and some visuals are being A/B tested.
  • External partnership development progressed:
    • Yallad: framework prepared and validated; the meeting should take place on 30 September.
    • WLB: discussions remain at the stage of assessing the most relevant partnership model.
    • AWB: presentation is ready to send; next step is to organise a meeting and agree on immediate quick wins.
    • Orange: partnership discussions remain in progress.

Plan of Action

Briefing & Production Audit
  • Establish briefing quality as the first operational improvement priority and compare the next audit against the current 30% baseline.
  • Continue auditing the full production funnel to identify additional sources of delay and measure productivity improvements week over week.
Activation Playbook
  • Implement the Activation Playbook across Marketing and progressively across other teams.
Sales on Monday
  • Complete Sales onboarding on Monday and align project-management expectations, ownership and reporting.
Partnerships
  • Hold the Yallad meeting and define the immediate implementation roadmap.
  • Send the AWB presentation, organise the working session and agree on quick-win opportunities.
  • Continue evaluating the WLB partnership model and progressing the Orange discussions.

Blocking Points

Blocking point
Briefing impact not yet measurable
The production bottleneck has been identified, but the full impact of briefing issues and the effectiveness of the corrective measures will only be measurable through the next WoW comparison.
