Weekly Highlights
Performance Marketing
  • Acquisition contracted sharply across the board: Active Users fell 37% to 3,896, Sessions fell 44% to 8.34K, and Installs fell 62% to 1,064, a much steeper pullback than the prior week.
  • Organic Installs held up better than paid, down 33% to 298, so organic's share of total installs rose to 28% even as overall acquisition volume dropped.
  • Add To Cart fell 69% to 61, well ahead of the 44% Sessions decline, pointing to a weaker browsing-to-cart rate on top of the smaller audience.
  • The funnel narrows hard at the top: only 0.7% of sessions reached Add to Cart, though the bottom held up better, with 16.4% of carts reaching checkout and 30.0% of checkouts converting to purchase, for 3 purchases from 10 checkout visits.
  • af_purchase unique users fell 90% to 3, the steepest decline of any tile on this page.
  • Budget Spent was not included in this week's AppsFlyer export, so that tile is omitted rather than carried over from W30.
Page 03 Overview / Ops

Overview & Ops

Weekly highlights across workstreams, the plan of action by team, and current blocking points. Source: Marketing and Brand teams, outside digital performance.

Weekly Highlights
  • Back-to-School Campaign: Preparation is ongoing across all workstreams. Ad assets, Morocco Mall screens, and campaign materials are in production. Offline activation remains pending budget approval and concept validation. A 3D mock-up has been requested to support the final decision, and concept development is underway.
  • Bad Fall Campaign: Budget finalized and submitted for validation. Urgent approval is required to maintain production timelines; otherwise, the activation will need to be cancelled.
  • Dynamic Product Ads (DPA): Successfully launched with a limited budget. Initial performance remains below expectations. Next phase will introduce creative-led DPA formats using video and stronger hooks instead of standard product packshots to improve engagement and CTR.
  • AI Production Workflows: Development remains blocked due to the absence of Claude AI accounts. A decision is required regarding AI tool access for the Marketing team to proceed with workflow automation.
  • Mystery Box: Campaign KV remains in production and is behind the agreed delivery timeline, delaying campaign preparation.
  • Brand Development: Team has been briefed. Owners, responsibilities, and deadlines have been assigned. Delivery is targeted for 31 August for final approval.
  • Affiliate Program: First strategic review completed. The second draft is currently being developed and is expected to be ready for presentation by the end of the week, pending final onboarding timelines from Azza.
