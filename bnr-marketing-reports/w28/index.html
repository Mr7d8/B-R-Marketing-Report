Weekly Highlights
Performance Marketing
  • Budget spent reached 2,416.79 EUR for the week, with 2,058 installs and 413 organic installs, an organic share of about 20%.
  • Sessions declined to 15K, down 25% week over week, with active users down 17% to 6,320, a broad softening across the top of funnel.
  • Add To Cart fell to 174 (down 30%), and the drop carried through to checkout, 27, and purchases, 8, down 68% week over week.
  • Retention held at 15.16% despite the volume decline, suggesting the users who did convert stayed engaged.

Overview & Ops

Weekly highlights across workstreams, the plan of action by team, and current blocking points.

Weekly Highlights
  • Shop N Shop: Delivered. Final reviews and finishing touches are underway.
  • AKSAL Employee Program: Proposal submitted to M. Boutaib for review.
  • Marketing Operations: New Marketing and Sales Campaign Process developed with the Organizational Team. Presentation scheduled for Tuesday.
  • Offline Campaign: Offline budget activated. Merhba50 flyer distribution launches this week alongside the Spin and Win activation, now featuring an electric bike as the grand prize.
  • OOH Campaign: 12-second Digital OOH campaign ready for deployment. Awaiting Home category products from Sales before final export. Planned delivery by end of day.
  • Back-to-School: Campaign Management Workbook finalized. Budget preparation and approval pending. Student Discount proposition prepared, mechanics to be validated.
  • Creator Activation: SIIF Wasalers received vouchers and unique tracking codes linked to the Merhba50 campaign to measure creator performance and attributed sales.
  • Social Media: Multiple giveaway activations scheduled throughout the SIIF campaign to maintain engagement, reach, and user acquisition momentum.
  • Creative Production: W Studio maintained consistent creative output. Dynamic Product Ads (DPA) are now live. AI-powered workflows for Trend Creatives, Product Stories, and Banner generation (FR / AR / EN) are being deployed by Badr and Zineb.

Plan of Action

Media Planning
  • Finalize the 2-month Media and Digital Marketing Strategy (today).
  • Confirm channel split, budgets, audience segmentation, A/B testing roadmap, and performance framework.
Mystery Box
  • Finalize P&L with COMEX.
  • Enter validation process with Inditex.
  • Finalize KV, campaign mechanics, budget, and offline/online activation plan.
  • Target pilot launch during the last week of July.
Brand Development
  • Continue Brand Guidelines review.
  • Finalize updated Tone of Voice.
  • Simplify Brand Guidelines for easier day-to-day operational use across teams.
Content Strategy
  • Maintain target of 120 W Moments per month, including seller-generated content.
  • Continue scaling organic content production and creator participation.
Marketing Automation
  • Automate W Moments moderation workflow.
  • Deploy AI-powered creative production workflows to increase W Studio output, speed, and multilingual production.
