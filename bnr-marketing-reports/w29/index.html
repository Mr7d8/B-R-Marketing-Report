Weekly Highlights
Performance Marketing
  • Budget spent reached €2,555.35 for the week, with Active Users up 15% to 7,307 and Sessions up 14% to 17.14K, broad growth across the top of funnel.
  • Organic Installs grew to 418 (+1%) and Add To Cart rose 12% to 209, both improving on last week's pace.
  • Mixpanel sessions were up 5.2% to 12,673, but Articles consultés fell 9.2% to 8,762 and Ajouts au panier slipped 1.0% to 1,278. The ATC/articles ratio still improved to 14.59% (+9.1%), so a smaller pool of browsers converted to cart at a higher rate.
  • Purchases (af_purchase, unique users) doubled to 16 (+100%), and Retention held at 13.81% despite the mixed mid-funnel signals, suggesting the users who did convert stayed engaged.
  • 28 unique users navigated to /checkout this week (AppsFlyer), for context alongside the 16 purchases.
