Weekly Highlights
Performance Marketing
  • Installs edged up 3% to 2,515, but the mix changed: organic installs jumped 61% to 619, or 24.6% of installs against 15.8% in W46. iOS drove it (463 organic installs against 239), while Android organic held at 156 (W46: 146).
  • Non-organic installs fell 8% to 1,896. Google Ads brought 1,190 (−26% from 1,607) and Facebook Ads 199 (from 307), partly offset by Shared links, which almost quadrupled to 472 (from 120).
  • Reach was flat but usage grew: 5,849 active users (+0.3%) opened 11,376 app sessions (+11%). Returning visitors made 68% of sessions (67% in W46) and the bounce rate fell to 14.4% from 17.4%.
  • Shopping intent rose sharply in the Wasal Friday week: sessions opening a product page +38% to 4,606, sessions with a successful add to cart +58% to 1,249 (11.0% of sessions against 7.8%), and checkout starts +69% to 579.
  • Purchase sessions more than doubled to 178 (W46: 84), lifting app session conversion to 1.56% from 0.82%. Ad spend is not in the lakehouse (Meta app cost stops on 25/09/2026), so there is no budget tile this week.
