Page 03 Overview / Ops

Overview & Ops

Outside digital performance: campaign, brand, content and production workstreams, the plan of action for the weeks ahead, and current blocking points.

Weekly Highlights
General overview
  • Black Friday / Wasal Friday preparation progressed, with the campaign identity, message matrix, creative direction and audience work continuing. Key campaign workstreams remain under pressure.
  • Product and commercial readiness advanced: several Sales × W-Studio deliverables for 25 September are approved and due soon.
  • Brand Guidelines 2.0 moved into finalisation, but the “What is Wasal?” presentation remains critical and requires rework. Social, CRM and cross-channel templates are approaching their 24–29 September deadlines.
  • Content production continued: several Moments are now in validation, while three social assets went live this week.
  • WFC production progressed: warehouse video editing and photo retouching were completed. A new photo/video shoot is currently in progress.
  • The Black Friday landing-page / product-experience workstream is on track and remains a critical priority.

Plan of Action

Brand Guidelines 2.0
  • Social, paid and catalogue templates, 24 September.
  • What is Wasal presentation, to be sent to our Partners.
  • CRM, print and presentation templates, 25 September.
  • Cross-channel consistency test, 25 September.
  • Master template library, 29 September.
Wasal Friday / Black Friday
  • Resolve blocked workstreams: consumer and commercial insights.
  • Continue the Black Friday landing-page brief, UX structure and first design direction.
Sales × W-Studio
  • Complete and approve the deliverables due 25 September, including autumn, fashion, tech, sport, home and children’s selections.
Content & Social
  • Validate pending Moments and replace the PlayStation 5 Moment, which is marked out of stock.
  • Clear the social-media backlog, prioritising assets marked Critical, Adjustment Needed, Blocked or Sales Validation.
Next Steps
  • Confirm next steps for Garnier catalogue / e-merchandising visuals.
  • Confirm next steps for the WFC website design.
