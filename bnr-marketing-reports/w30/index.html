Weekly Highlights
Performance Marketing
  • Acquisition contracted across the board: Active Users fell 15% to 6,190, AppsFlyer Sessions fell 13% to 14.88K and Installs fell 16% to 2,799, reversing the broad growth recorded in W29.
  • Organic Installs were the one acquisition bright spot, up 6% to 444, so the decline sits in paid and returning traffic rather than in organic discovery.
  • The Mixpanel funnel narrowed at every stage: Sessions -11.8% to 11,158, Articles consultés -5.1% to 8,317 and Ajouts au panier -8.0% to 1,176. Browsing intent held up best, with 74.5% of sessions reaching a product page.
  • Cart conversion softened slightly: the Ajouts au panier / Articles consultés ratio slipped 3.1% to 14.14%, so the smaller audience added to cart at a marginally lower rate than in W29.
  • Achats came in at 41, converting 3.5% of carts. AppsFlyer separately reports 28 af_purchase unique users (+56%) against 42 unique users reaching /checkout, a 66.7% checkout completion rate.
  • Retention and Budget Spent were not included in this week's AppsFlyer export, so both tiles are omitted rather than carried over from W29.
Page 03 Overview / Ops

Overview & Ops

Weekly highlights across workstreams, the plan of action by team, and current blocking points. Source: Marketing and Brand teams, outside digital performance.

Weekly Highlights
  • Back-to-School Campaign: Morocco Mall offline activation budget completed and ready for validation.
  • Bad Fall Back to School Collab: Budget finalized and ready for validation.
  • Paid Media Assets: Creative briefs completed. Ad production is underway across campaign formats.
  • Dynamic Product Ads (DPA): Full deployment has not yet been achieved. Investigation is ongoing to identify and resolve the issue.
  • August Content Strategy: Content plan reviewed and refined with a stronger focus on conversion-driven content, product placement, and performance KPIs.
  • OOH Campaign: 12-second Digital OOH campaign successfully deployed.
  • AI Production Workflows: AI-powered workflows for creative production remain under development and have not yet been deployed.
  • 2-Hour Delivery Experience: UX/UI audit of the H+2 customer journey is underway to identify friction points and improve conversion.
  • Mystery Box: Awaiting P&L validation. Due to timing, there is a low probability of launching during the initial phase of the Back-to-School campaign. Campaign KV is currently in production.
  • Brand Development: Brand Guidelines update has become a critical priority due to increasing inconsistencies in day-to-day execution.
  • Affiliate Program: Annual deployment strategy and Wasaler onboarding roadmap are being developed. Draft V1 will be presented next week. Azza will support onboarding analysis to identify operational bottlenecks, as current onboarding pace presents a risk to annual acquisition targets.

Plan of Action

Campaign Execution
  • Obtain validation for the Back-to-School Offline Activation and Bad Fall campaign budgets.
  • Finalize and launch campaign creative assets across all paid media channels.
Performance & Digital
  • Resolve the remaining DPA deployment issues.
  • Complete the UX/UI audit of the 2-Hour Delivery customer journey and prioritize quick-win improvements.
Mystery Box
  • Finalize P&L validation with COMEX.
  • Complete campaign KV and mechanics.
  • Assess revised launch timing depending on approval status.
Brand Development
  • Finalize the updated Brand Guidelines and Tone of Voice documentation to ensure consistency across all marketing outputs.
Affiliate Program
  • Present Version 1 of the annual Wasaler acquisition and onboarding strategy.
  • Review onboarding process with Azza to identify friction points and accelerate creator acquisition.
Marketing Automation
  • Continue development of AI-powered creative production workflows to increase W Studio capacity and multilingual asset production.

Blocking Points

Campaign Budget Approvals

Validation pending for the Back-to-School Morocco Mall Activation and Bad Fall campaign budgets.

Affiliate Program

Auto-Entrepreneur legal status remains unresolved, delaying creator onboarding. Current onboarding pace presents a risk to achieving annual Wasaler acquisition targets.

Mystery Box

P&L validation is still pending, delaying production, packing, and campaign launch.

Brand Guidelines

Updated Brand Guidelines require urgent completion. The absence of a finalized framework is creating inconsistencies across creative production and campaign execution.

Content Production Budget

40,000 MAD cash budget remains required for upcoming video productions. Current social media content is experiencing creative fatigue, and new hero assets are needed to support upcoming campaigns, engagement, and paid media performance.
