Weekly Highlights
Performance Marketing
  • Top of funnel contracted across the board: Active Users fell 15% to 6,190, Sessions fell 13% to 14.88K, and Installs fell 16% to 2,799, reversing the broad growth recorded in W29.
  • Organic Installs were the one acquisition bright spot, up 6% to 444, so the decline sits in paid and returning traffic rather than in organic discovery.
  • Add To Cart fell 16% to 182, tracking the session decline almost exactly, which points to less traffic rather than a worse browsing-to-cart experience.
  • Purchases (af_purchase, unique users) rose 56% to 28 despite the smaller top of funnel, the second consecutive week of purchase growth and the strongest signal in the week.
  • 42 unique users reached /checkout against 28 purchases, a 66.7% checkout-to-purchase completion rate, and 23.1% of carts reached checkout.
  • Retention and Budget Spent were not included in this week's AppsFlyer export, so both tiles are omitted rather than carried over from W29.
